# Veto Terms of Use (plain English)

*Last updated: 2026-09-14. This is a plain-English summary of the deal. It is not
legal advice, and a lawyer should review it before Veto's public launch.*

## The deal

**Veto is free.** In exchange, Veto sends anonymous usage data and problem reports
back to the Veto team so we can find bugs and make the product better. That's the
trade-off: you get a free job-search machine, we get the data we need to improve it.

If you're not comfortable with that, Veto isn't for you — please don't use it.

## What gets sent

- **Crash and problem reports.** When something breaks, Veto automatically sends a
  report so we can fix it. Before anything leaves your machine, personal information
  is scrubbed out on your device. You can preview exactly what was sent.
- **Anonymous usage analytics.** Which features get used, how often, and performance
  data (e.g., how long a search takes). Counts and timings — never the content of
  what you were doing.
- **Feedback you choose to send.** Anything you type into "Report a problem."

## What is NEVER sent

- Your resume, cover letters, or application materials.
- Your emails or messages.
- Your name, email address, or anything that identifies you.
- Anything typed into a job application.

Scrubbing happens on your device, before data leaves it. If the scrubber can't prove
something is clean, it doesn't get sent.

## Where it goes

Problem reports become issues on Veto's GitHub repository so the team can track and
fix them. Analytics are aggregated — we look at trends across all users, never at
individuals. We don't sell your data. There is no advertising in Veto.

## Your control

- You see the full terms and accept them before first use. No silent opt-in.
- You can view every report Veto has sent from your machine, any time, in Settings.
- Telemetry is part of the free deal and can't be turned off while using the free
  version. (If that changes, these terms will change first, and you'll be told.)

## If the deal changes

We'll update this file and tell you in the app before the new terms apply. Continued
use after that means you accept the new deal.
