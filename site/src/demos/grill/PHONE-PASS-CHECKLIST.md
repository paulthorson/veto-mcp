# Phone-pass checklist — Chakra features (A–H) on the immersive demo

Branch: `demo/immersive-grill-2026-10-01`. Open the demo on your phone (preview link TBD — Vercel access is a separate thread). Each item: do it, confirm it feels right. Nothing here is final until you say so.

## A. Reasoning narrative
- [ ] Run the Standard grill to the verdict. Each verdict shows a "how they reasoned" trace, not just pass/fail.
- [ ] The trace reads like what you actually did (approach, key decisions), not generic filler.

## B. AI fluency score
- [ ] Verdict shows an AI-fluency score next to the fit score, with Framing / Judgment / Steering bars.
- [ ] Bars use the green → amber → red gradient (same as the Radar).

## C. Brutal defend-your-answer
- [ ] Run Brutal. After the grill, it pushes back on your answers and makes you defend them.
- [ ] It feels like a real interviewer pressing, not a quiz.

## D. Real-repo task
- [ ] One grill task sends you into a small realistic repo (acme-api fixture) instead of a toy puzzle.
- [ ] The task is solvable from the repo alone, no outside context needed.

## E. Feed it a job posting
- [ ] Paste a real job posting at the start. Questions reference its requirements; the verdict judges fit against that role.
- [ ] Try pasting "n/a" — the grill should fall back to generic questions, not break.

## F. Companion loop + pipeline board
- [ ] The pipeline board (wishlist → applied → interviewing → offer) shows your prospects at a glance.
- [ ] Stale prospects are flagged ("quiet 12 days"). Warm paths show who you know at each company, closest first.

## G. Resume match
- [ ] Paste your resume. It scores against the fed posting and names the gaps in plain English.
- [ ] The grill then drills you on those gaps.

## H. Delivery coaching
- [ ] Answer with filler words ("um", "like", "I think maybe"). The verdict coaches HOW you answered — filler count, hedging, pacing.
- [ ] Coaching is direct, never shaming.

## General
- [ ] No lorem-ipsum, no broken layouts on a phone screen.
- [ ] The whole flow still feels like Veto: direct, terminal-style, no brochure-speak.
